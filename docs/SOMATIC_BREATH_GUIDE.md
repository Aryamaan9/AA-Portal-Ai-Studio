# Somatic Nervous System Breath Guide

When an ambitious mind is in fight-or-flight mode, thinking your way out often makes the spiral worse. You need to physiologically down-regulate your nervous system first.

---

## 🌬️ How to Use the Breathing Tool

1. Click the **Wind / Breathe** icon in the header (on desktop) or tap **Breathe** in the bottom navigation (on mobile).
2. Follow the gently expanding and contracting circle:
   * **Inhale gently (4 seconds)**: Breathe in through your nose, letting your belly expand.
   * **Hold softly (4 seconds)**: Rest in the fullness of your lungs.
   * **Exhale completely (4 seconds)**: Release all air and tension slowly through your mouth.
   * **Hold peacefully (4 seconds)**: Sit in the still pause before the next breath.
3. Complete 3–5 cycles (1–2 minutes) until your heart rate slows and your body feels anchored.
4. Click **Finished Grounding** to return to your sanctuary.
