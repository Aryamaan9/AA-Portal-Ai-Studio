# Life Horizons & Plans Guide

The **Horizons** space replaces overwhelming corporate project backlogs with a calm, psychologically grounded 3-horizon mental model.

---

## 🎯 The 3 Horizons

### 1. Current Season (Next 30–90 Days)
* **What it is**: Your 2–3 essential commitments for the current season of your life.
* **Integrated Actions & To-Dos**: Capture high-leverage tasks with priorities (`Priority`, `Normal`, `Low`) and due dates.
* **Instant Completion**: 1-click checkmark toggle with quiet feedback.

### 2. Someday & Ideas
* **What it is**: A creative incubator for startup concepts, books to read, places to explore, and future possibilities without pressure.

### 3. North Star Values
* **What it is**: 3–5 foundational life principles and identity anchors for who Aryamaan is becoming.

---

## 📱 WhatsApp & ChatGPT Sync
* When you text *"Todo: Call accountant tomorrow"* on WhatsApp, it automatically lands in your **Season Action Items**!
* When you ask ChatGPT *"What are my current goals?"*, it reads directly from this Horizons engine.
