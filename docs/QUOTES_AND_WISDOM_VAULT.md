# Wisdom & Quotes Vault Guide

Your **Wisdom Vault** is your personal treasury of grounding lines, timeless philosophy, and motivational perspective.

---

## 📖 Key Features

### 1. Adding a New Quote
* **Desktop**: Click the **+ Capture** button in the top right (or press `Cmd + K` on Mac, `Ctrl + K` on Windows), or click **+ Add Quote** inside the Wisdom Vault.
* **Mobile**: Tap the **+** button at the bottom center of your screen.
* **Fields**:
  * **Quote / Wisdom Line** (Required): The words that moved you.
  * **Author / Voice**: Who said or wrote it (e.g. *Marcus Aurelius*, *Naval*, *Seneca*, *Buddha*, or *Self*).
  * **Source / Context**: Book, podcast, date, or walk context (e.g. *Meditations*, *Walking in the evening*).
  * **Pillars & Moods**: Tap tag pills like `Perspective`, `Peace`, `Resilience`, `Focus`, `Letting Go`, or type your own.
  * **Personal Context**: A private note on why this matters to you.
  * **Pin as Daily Anchor**: Check this to make it your featured quote on the home screen.

---

### 2. Daily Anchor & Home Rotation
* Any quote you bookmark with the gold bookmark icon becomes your **Daily Anchor**.
* It appears prominently at the top of your **Sanctuary Home** screen.
* If you have multiple quotes, clicking **Another** softly rotates through your collection.

---

### 3. Fullscreen Contemplate Mode
* Click on any quote card or Daily Anchor to enter **Contemplate Mode**.
* This opens an ultra-clean, full-screen view with large, peaceful serif typography.
* Perfect for sitting quietly with a single thought during morning coffee or before bed.

---

### 4. Search & Filtering
* **Instant Search**: Type any word, author name, or book title in the search bar to filter instantly.
* **Tag Filters**: Click on any tag pill (e.g. `#Peace`, `#Resilience`) at the top of the vault to see only quotes related to that theme.
