# Mind Mirror & Emotional Grounding Guide

The **Mind Mirror** is your private sanctuary space for unburdening your mind when you are feeling anxious, overwhelmed, scattered, or stuck.

---

## 🧠 How It Works

1. **Speak Without Filter**:
   * Type whatever is on your mind into the box: *"I feel like I'm falling behind on my goals and my mind is racing."*
   * Or tap one of the quick mood suggestion pills if you are too tired to write:
     * *Overwhelmed with decisions*
     * *Anxious about the future*
     * *Feeling scattered & unfocused*
     * *Carrying heavy fatigue*
     * *Lost momentum*
     * *Quietly grateful*

2. **Click "Reflect & Ground"**:
   * The system immediately analyzes the underlying emotional state.
   * It provides:
     1. **A Calm Grounding Reframe**: Rooted in Acceptance & Commitment Therapy (ACT), Stoicism, and Somatic regulation to calm your nervous system.
     2. **A Socratic Inquiry Question**: One clear, gentle question to help de-catastrophize and regain clarity.
     3. **Relevant Wisdom Anchors**: Automatically searches your Wisdom Vault for quotes and principles you previously saved that apply to this exact feeling.
