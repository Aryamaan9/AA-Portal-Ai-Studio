# Diary & Reflections Journal Guide

The **Diary** space is a distraction-free, quiet digital room designed for unburdening your mind, journaling, and tracking insights.

---

## 🌿 Key Features

1. **Quiet Writing & Prompt Inspiration**:
   - Includes gentle prompt starters: *Morning Clarity*, *Evening Unwind*, *Honest Unburden*, *Gratitude Anchor*.
2. **Multi-Source Timeline**:
   - Entries captured via **WhatsApp** or saved through **ChatGPT** automatically appear with gentle badges (`WhatsApp`, `ChatGPT`, `Web`).
3. **Tagging & Instant Search**:
   - Filter by tags (*Reflection*, *Gratitude*, *Work*, *Health*, *Peace*) or search across all entries instantly.
4. **Local-First & Cloud Sync**:
   - Stored safely in your private browser storage, with optional real-time sync to Supabase.
